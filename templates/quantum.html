🔬 Quantum Hardware Constraints

NISQ Era Reality

Simulating quantum algorithms on classical CPU backends scales exponentially ($O(2^N)$ statevector memory and $O(4^N)$ gate matrix operations).

On genuine NISQ quantum hardware (IBM Quantum / IonQ), decoherence time ($T_2$), two-qubit gate error rates (~0.1% to 1%), and readout infidelity present significant hurdles compared to perfect noiseless mathematical simulation.
